How it's made

Everything here is generated in the browser. There are no models and no textures, only noise, a few formulas and one HTML file.

Stone

Each tower is a cylinder with 64 rings and 72 segments. A sugarloaf profile shapes it, waterline erosion undercuts it, and noise sampled around the ring carves vertical flutes. The stone shader adds drip stains, rust streaks, bedding bands and moss on every surface that faces the sky.

Water

The scene is rendered once into an HDR target with depth. The water then reads that depth to measure its own thickness, and colour is absorbed through that thickness (Beer–Lambert). Screen-space refraction bends what lies beneath, and a 28-step ray march mirrors the towers. Ripples come from a wave equation solved on the GPU at 256×256.

Mist

Six drifting noise layers fade softly wherever they meet stone. A 128×128 clearing mask records where your cursor has been and fills back in over a few seconds.

Light

A sun moves from 05:30 to 19:30 and sets the mist, sky reflection and lantern glow. The final pass applies ACES tone mapping and a gentle tilt-shift so the scene reads as a miniature.

Raft

A bamboo raft with seven poles, a fisherman in a straw hat, two cormorants and a bow lantern. It loops down one side of the channel and back up the other. Towers push it away and so do whiskers that test for shallow water. It bobs on the same wave formula the water shader uses, so it floats in step with the surface. Click the water to send it there.

Shark

A small, playful river shark: a lathe body, extruded fins and a vertex shader that bends it into an S-curve which speeds up as it swims. Use WASD to swim, Space to burst, Shift to dive and Q to surface. Near the surface its fin cuts a V-shaped wake into the ripple simulation and a channel through the mist. Pass the raft and the cormorants take off.

Underwater

When the chase camera dips below the surface, the renderer switches paths. Jade fog swallows the walls of the block, shafts of light slant down from the surface, and motes drift past. Seen from below, the surface is a mirror with one bright Snell's window to the sky.
